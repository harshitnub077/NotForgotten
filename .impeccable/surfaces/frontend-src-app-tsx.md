Scope: the whole NotForgotten web app (Operate) plus its landing page (Persuade, same world).
Audience: exam students with a date; daily 5–20 minute sessions, laptop + phone.
Task: know what to study next and what it costs; do it; trust the plan to exam day.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Studying is making your connection. Every screen is a terminal sign system: one yellow band says the next decision and its cost in minutes, the exam is the departure. Refuses the category default of a sidebar + stat-card SaaS dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Signal yellow #FFCC00 owns wayfinding bands, nav and the next action; sign black #0B0B0B for type, pictogram insets and live departure boards (yellow/white split-flap mono on black); surface gray #E6E6E6 and white information boards for reading. Memory state is flight status words (ON TIME, BOARDING, FINAL CALL, MISSED) plus the number, never colour alone. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (humanist, signage-legible) with its Mono for boards. Square-ish 4px corners, 1px rules, arrows locked to the edge they point past.

STORY: The student sees "Review · 14 cards · 9 min →", believes the plan, does the session, watches concepts move from FINAL CALL to ON TIME, and checks the exam departure is on track.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Today: left yellow sign rail (pictogram squares + labels); a monumental band top-left "Next up: Review" with card count at sign scale and minutes underneath and the black arrow button; right, a black departure board ranking concepts by recall with status words; below, three wayfinding tiles (exam countdown, streak, retention).

FORM: Terminal Wayfinding (airport sign system), challenger chosen by user over assigned Highlighted Textbook; seed key a6ea7bfc. Signature interaction: split-flap board updates when ratings change memory state; time-machine slider re-boards the knowledge graph.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
